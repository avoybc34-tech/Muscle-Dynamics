Exact resolutions are in the lockfiles; preserve upstream notices and perform a release-specific license review before distributing native products. This is not a claim of a completed legal/license audit.

Demo people, prices, progress and memberships are illustrative and isolated from live mode. No real bank accounts or customer records are supplied. The demo's displayed training content is not individualized medical advice.

No external font/photo CDN is required by the app. No private production signing keys, Apple provisioning profiles or provider service-account files are included. Native test signing is intentionally not a production identity.
