# Dependencies and sample artwork

Expo, React, React Native, expo-font, expo-system-ui, expo-status-bar, safe-area-context and Ionicons retain their upstream licenses. Exact versions/resolution are in package.json and package-lock.json. Preserve upstream notices when distributing native products; the lockfile is not a release-specific license review.

The MD icons are simple project typographic artwork; trademark ownership is not asserted. The bundled sample receipt is locally rendered placeholder artwork labelled SAMPLE / NOT A PAYMENT. All people, prices, membership records and support text are clearly identified illustrative examples. No bank accounts or real customer records are supplied.

The generated Android test keystore is publicly known and is not a production secret. No private signing certificates/keys or Apple provisioning profiles are included in this source ZIP.
